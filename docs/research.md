# Portfolio and project research

Observed November 18, 2026. I screened **33 distinct creator portfolios or project collections**. I found **31 through publicly traceable X posts or their archives and indexed references**, plus two supplemental leads. Ten produced a project-level comparison. Four older portfolio URLs or replacements remained inaccessible. The table distinguishes the original observations from archive-only evidence. I searched for portfolio launches, review threads, data dashboards, operations tools, open-source demos and known creator launches. The broad X searches returned finance results and login pages, and individual post opens often failed. Public archives, creator project pages and source repositories supplied the evidence below. Current X engagement and reply chains remain unverified for most posts. Counts transcribed from a mirror are labeled as such and may reflect its crawl time.

## Finalist scorecard

Scores out of 5. Weighted totals out of 100. Verified success was penalized where evidence came from creators or vendor case studies.

| Criterion | Weight | Cal.com → staffing triage | Datasette → exception review | Remote OK → eligible applicant shortlist |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Business decision | 30% | 5 · Escalate an uncovered role on a deadline | 4 · Sort and inspect operational exceptions, with a domain policy still needed | 4 · Filter jobs by pay and jurisdiction |
| Engineering | 30% | 5 · Interdependent skills, assignments and hard cap | 4 · Query provenance and validation | 3 · Normalization, eligibility and ranking |
| Success evidence | 20% | 4 · Named team adoption reported by vendor | 4 · External archive deployment | 3 · Founder retrospective and public board |
| Free demo | 10% | 5 · Static fixtures and client-side solver | 4 · Static extracts or browser database | 4 · Static data feasible and live feed optional |
| Original variation | 10% | 5 · Budget and criticality change the workflow materially | 4 · Decision-specific review instead of generic browsing | 4 · Auditable eligibility for a defined persona |
| **Weighted total** | **100%** | **96** | **80** | **70** |

Cal.com advances because each staffing assignment changes the remaining feasible assignments. The decision and optimization challenge are visible in a four-role example. Datasette has stronger independent deployment evidence, while its proposed exception-review variation needs additional domain policy to produce an equally concrete decision.

**Variation.** The original helps teams route bookings to available people. My version helps a food-bank shift coordinator decide which open roles to cover or escalate by adding qualifications, ranked coverage priorities, and a hard labor cap.

The inspiration is conceptual. No Cal.com code, content or assets are included. The sample needs no public dataset, dependency credentials or paid runtime.
